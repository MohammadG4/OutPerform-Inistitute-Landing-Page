Structured Operating Path

Three phases to predictable revenue.

Every engagement follows a disciplined progression: audit root causes first, embed operational leadership to institute structure, then deploy automated system intelligence for ongoing execution.

01
Phase 01 · Diagnostic Starting Point

Sales System Audit

A rigorous, evidence-based diagnostic of your current sales mechanics. Before changing team behaviors, we inspect deal velocity, pipeline stage definitions, and forecast accuracy to pinpoint structural points of failure.

Audit Deliverables

  • Revenue Leak Map — quantitative breakdown of stalled deals and cycle delays.
  • Pipeline Stage Inspection — verification of buyer actions versus rep assumptions.
  • Rep Activity Audit — objective measurement of activity compliance and deal hygiene.

Executive Outcome

You receive an objective diagnostic report identifying why performance is inconsistent, exactly where revenue is being lost, and the explicit structural blueprint required to fix it.

PHASE 01 / 03
02
Phase 02 · Embedded Execution

Fractional Sales Leadership

Embedded operational leadership dedicated one day per week to your business. We step into the sales manager role, taking full accountability for rep discipline, pipeline inspection, and revenue execution.

Core Architecture

  • 1 Embedded Day / Week — direct management cadence and deal inspection.
  • 30-Day Revenue Control Brief — immediate establishment of objective forecast rules.
  • 90-Day Operating Plan — complete VP-of-Sales-ready documentation and playbook.

Operating Guarantee

If the 30-Day Revenue Control Brief and weekly inspection cadence are not fully delivered, the next month of embedded leadership is provided at zero cost.

03
Phase 03 · Continuous Software Layer

OutPerform Revenue Intelligence

The permanent system layer left behind to safeguard process compliance. Integrates with your existing CRM to continuously monitor rep activity, detect process drift, and trigger automated leadership alerts before deals stall.

System Capabilities

  • CRM Integration — automated tracking of deal velocity and stage gates.
  • Process Drift Alerts — real-time notifications when rep behavior strays.
  • Objective Pipeline Scorecard — data-backed forecast inspection panel.

Long-Term Outcome

Your sales system maintains high execution standards indefinitely — preventing the company from slipping back into founder-dependent sales management.
