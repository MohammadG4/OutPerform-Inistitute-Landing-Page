Root Cause Analysis

Sales Performance is a System Problem. Not a Training Problem.

When a founder steps back from direct sales management, revenue inconsistency follows. Without explicit operating architecture, sales representatives default to ad hoc habits.

PROBLEM // 01

Dependency on Individuals

Your revenue depends heavily on one or two top performers. When reps execute their own custom pitches, performance varies wildly and outcomes become unpredictable.

PROBLEM // 02

Subjective Pipeline Metrics

Pipeline stages are based on rep sentiment rather than verifiable buyer action. Forecast reviews become storytelling sessions instead of objective data inspection.

PROBLEM // 03

Founder Bottleneck

You are required in every complex deal to push it across the finish line. Your team cannot close independently because the operating architecture does not exist.

Tactics & Isolated Pep Talks

Attempting to fix revenue issues with short-term advice or pep talks. Treats symptoms while leaving the underlying lack of structure untouched.

Structure & System Architecture

Designing an embedded sales operating system with strict inspection rules, defined stage gates, and objective accountability standards.
